Privacy policy

Casse-Glace, a game by Kanch Games

Last updated: October 25, 2026 (version 1.0.0 of the game)

In short

Casse-Glace collects no personal data and sends nothing off your device. There is no account, no internet connection, no advertising and no analytics.

No account, no data collected or shared

The game asks for no account, no sign-up and no email address. It collects no personal data and shares no data, neither with us nor with anyone else: it contains no analytics, audience measurement, advertising or tracking tool, and sets no cookies.

No network permission

The game asks for no network permission: it cannot connect to the internet. The only permission it asks Android for is vibration (VIBRATE), for small vibrations while playing; they can be turned off in the game's settings.

What is saved on your device

So that you can pick up where you left off, the game saves, only in its own storage on your device:

  • your progress: puzzles solved and their stars, daily challenge history and streaks, crystals, hints, whether the tutorial was seen;
  • your settings (sound, vibration, view, language, graphics quality, mood…);
  • the game in progress, to resume it;
  • for multiplayer, the first names you choose to type for the players (optional: otherwise “Player 1”, “Player 2”…) and the multiplayer game in progress.

This data stays on the device: we have no access to it. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for this game; the data only leaves the device if you copy it yourself straight to a new device (Android's device-to-device transfer).

Deleting this data

Uninstalling the game deletes all of this data. You can also erase your progress at any time from the game's settings (“Reset progress”); your settings are then kept.

Children and families

Casse-Glace is for everyone, children included. It is designed in line with Google Play's policies for apps for children and families. Links that leave the game (this policy, the contact email) are behind a parental gate: a multiplication to solve.

Changes

If this policy changes (for instance with a new version of the game), the new version will be published on this page with its update date.
