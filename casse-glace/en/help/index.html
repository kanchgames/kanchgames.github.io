How to play

  • Swipe a penguin in a direction: it slides in a straight line until it hits a wall, the edge of the board, or another penguin. It can't stop halfway.
  • The card at the top of the screen shows the goal: bring the penguin of the right color to its fish. The rainbow fish takes any penguin.
  • The penguin must stop on the fish: sliding over it doesn't count.
  • Each slide counts as one move, whichever penguin moves. Moving another penguin to give it something to stop against is often the key.
  • Three stars for the fewest moves possible, two with one move more, one beyond that.
  • “Undo” takes back a move, “Restart” starts the puzzle over. Pinch to zoom; the view button switches between the tilted view and the view from above.

The modes

Play

Three levels: easy (2 to 4 moves), medium (5 to 7) and expert (8 and more). Each is a track of numbered levels, from the simplest to the hardest, opening in order: beating a level opens the next one. You can replay any level you've done to earn more stars.

Daily challenge

A new puzzle every day (at midnight, universal time), the same for everyone. Only your first try of the day counts for your streak. No hints during the challenge.

Multiplayer

Lay the tablet flat between the players (2 to 4). Each one has a corner. When the fish drops, think it through in your head: the first to find a way bids their number of moves and the hourglass starts. While it runs, everyone may bid in turn or lower their bid. Then the lowest bid shows its solution; if it works, that player wins the catch. A slip of the finger? During their demonstration, a player may “Start over” once: the penguins go back to the start and the count goes back to zero. The first to reach the chosen number of catches wins. Each player may also press “I pass” (without a bid) or “I'm done” (after bidding): once everyone is ready, the hourglass stops; if everyone passes without a bid, the solution shows and the next fish comes.

Multiplayer is unlocked by a one-time purchase, “Casse-Glace Full”, at the price shown by Google Play; a grown-up first answers the parental gate.

Crystals and hints

Each puzzle solved earns crystals by its stars (more in expert), and winning the daily challenge gives a bonus. The game screen's “Help” button offers three hints paid in crystals: the fewest moves, the key penguins, and the next move. You can also see the solution. Stuck on a level? “Skip this level” (15 crystals) opens the next one; you can always come back and beat it for real.

Frequently asked questions

Can my progress move to another device?

It is saved only on the device, with no account and no cloud backup; only Android's device-to-device transfer can copy it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

How do I start over?

In Settings, “Reset progress” (press twice to confirm). Your settings and your purchase are kept.

I reinstalled the game or changed devices: how do I get my purchase back?

It is linked to your Google account: the game finds it by itself at start-up. Otherwise, in Settings, press “Restore my purchases”.

The game stutters on my device.

In Settings, pick a lower “Graphics quality”, or turn on “Reduced animations”.

There is no sound.

Check “Music” and “Effects” in the settings. When the phone is on silent or vibrate, the game is silent too.
