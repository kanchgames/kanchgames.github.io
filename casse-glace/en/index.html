Casse-Glace

Slide the penguins across the ice and find the shortest way.

How it works

On a 16 × 16 ice floe, four penguins (red, blue, green and yellow) slide in a straight line and only stop against a wall, at the edge, or against another penguin. Bring the right penguin to its fish in as few slides as you can: each slide counts as one move, and you may move any penguin to give it something to stop against.

Play alone or with the family

  • 5,000 puzzles on three levels: easy (2 to 4 moves), medium (5 to 7) and expert (8 and more).
  • Daily challenge: a new puzzle every day, the same for everyone, with your streak of days won.
  • Multiplayer on one tablet, 2 to 4 players: from their own corner, each player bids how many moves they need, the hourglass runs, and the lowest bid shows how it's done.
  • An animated tutorial: a penguin guide shows each move before you try.
  • Crystals earned by playing, to get hints.
  • Four moods by the time of day: day, sunset, northern lights, and now and then a snowstorm.
  • No account, no connection needed: everything stays on the device.
  • In English and in French.

Availability

Casse-Glace is being tested on Android.
